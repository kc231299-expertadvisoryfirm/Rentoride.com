Frequently Asked Questions

How do I rent a bike or car? +

Choose a vehicle, complete your profile and verification, select your date and time, then complete the payment.

Verification helps create a safer rental experience for customers and vehicle owners.

The available pickup or delivery option depends on what the vehicle owner has enabled.

Can I cancel my booking? +

Cancellation and refund eligibility will depend on the final RentoRide cancellation policy.

Available payment methods will be shown during the final payment step.
